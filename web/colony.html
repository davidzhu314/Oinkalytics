Who's calling?

A colony of ten young Egyptian fruit bats, and a model that learned their voices. Every call you hear here was recorded on a day the model never heard. It can't tell whether they're fighting (we checked, and the clock knew more than the model), but it can often tell who's talking.

Listening…

Press Play a mystery call. You'll hear a real call from one of the ten bats, then see who the model thinks made it. Bats are picked in proportion to how often they really called, like a live colony; choose a bat below to test a quiet one.

How well it knows each bat

On every call from the six days it never heard. Chatty bats are easy; the quiet ones barely called on those days, and the model rarely gets them.

Data: Prat, Taub, Pratt & Yovel 2017, "An annotated dataset of Egyptian fruit bat vocalizations across varying contexts and during vocal ontogeny", Scientific Data; Figshare (CC0). Ten bats born in a lab colony at Tel Aviv University, about 7–9 months old, housed in groups of five. Model: Perch 2.0 embeddings + logistic regression, trained on 22 days and tested on 6 held-out days. Recorded at 250 kHz and downsampled, so you hear only the part of each call below 16 kHz. Part of what the model hears may be which chamber a bat lived in.
